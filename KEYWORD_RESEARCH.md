# UNIQOVA — Strategic Keyword Research & Intent Strategy

**Target Market:** India (Tier 1 & Tier 2 e-commerce hubs: Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Jaipur, Surat, Ahmedabad) with international expansion (US, UK, UAE seller exports).  
**Core Business Priority:** E-commerce Management & Marketplace Growth (Amazon, Flipkart, Multi-channel, Cataloging, PPC).  
**Methodology:** Intent clustering, SERP competitive analysis, commercial intent classification, and topical authority mapping.

---

## 1. Keyword Strategy Overview

Search queries in the digital agency and marketplace management landscape divide into three key intent stages:

1. **Transactional / Commercial High-Intent (Bottom of Funnel - BOFU):**
   - Direct service searches where the prospect has budget and is actively seeking a trusted agency partner.
   - Examples: *"Amazon account management services India"*, *"hire Flipkart seller management agency"*, *"Amazon PPC management agency India"*.
   - **Target Pages:** Dedicated Service & Sub-Service Landing Pages.

2. **Investigation / Solution-Seeking (Middle of Funnel - MOFU):**
   - Searchers evaluating solutions to solve specific bottlenecks (high ACoS, listing suppression, low conversion rate).
   - Examples: *"how to lower Amazon ACoS fast"*, *"Flipkart listing optimization checklist"*, *"Next.js vs Shopify for e-commerce website"*.
   - **Target Pages:** Case Studies, Detailed Service Comparison Guides, Solution Overviews.

3. **Informational / Authority-Building (Top of Funnel - TOFU):**
   - Guides, marketplace policy updates, seller tutorials that attract organic backlink velocity and build trust.
   - Examples: *"Amazon brand registry India requirements 2026"*, *"Flipkart commission structure guide"*, *"mobile app development cost in India"*.
   - **Target Pages:** Educational Blog Hub (`/blog`).

## 3. Keyword Cannibalization Prevention Guidelines

To prevent two pages on UNIQOVA from competing for identical search queries in Google's index, strict keyword boundaries are established:

1. **Amazon Management vs Amazon PPC:**
   - `/services/amazon-account-management` targets end-to-end seller operations (BuyBox, account health, inventory, listing creation, FBA logistics).
   - `/services/amazon-ppc-management` exclusively targets paid advertising (Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Display, TACoS/ACoS optimization, keyword bidding).

2. **E-commerce General vs Specific Marketplaces:**
   - `/services/ecommerce` targets multi-channel brand scaling, direct-to-consumer strategy, and cross-platform synergy.
   - Dedicated sub-pages target specific platform mechanics (`/services/amazon-account-management`, `/services/flipkart-account-management`).

3. **Graphic Design vs Product Image Design:**
   - `/services/graphic-design` provides the macro umbrella for visual assets.
   - Product creative and Amazon listing imagery is internally linked and cross-referenced with appropriate canonical priority.

## 4. International vs Indian Search Intent Nuances

- **Indian Domestic Sellers:** Emphasize Flipkart & Amazon.in integration, GST compliance, regional fulfillment centers, Hindi/English multilingual listing nuances, and festive season scaling (Big Billion Days, Great Indian Festival).
- **Indian Global Sellers (Export):** Emphasize Amazon Global Selling (US, UK, UAE, EU), cross-border logistics, currency conversions, and international trademark/Brand Registry navigation.
- **International Brands Entering India:** Emphasize local regulatory navigation, Indian consumer buying behavior, localized price-point testing, and marketplace partner expertise.
